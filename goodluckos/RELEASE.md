Luanti for goodluckOS, as an app: the engine of this tag, Minetest Game and the mods that bring it close to Minecraft.

1. Copy `Luanti-*.zip` to the `apps` folder of the console's HOME (over USB, where the console shows up as a drive, or with the card in a card reader).
2. On the console, open System Settings -> System -> App Installer and install Luanti. It shows up in the Games tab.

A newer zip updates it the same way. Worlds and settings stay in `HOME/.minetest`. It needs a goodluckOS with the App Installer. How it plays, its controls and its mods: [goodluckos/README.md](https://github.com/alvesmaicon/luanti/blob/handheld/5.18/goodluckos/README.md).
