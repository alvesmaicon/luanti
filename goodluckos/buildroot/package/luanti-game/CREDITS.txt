Minetest Game and the mods goodluckOS adds to it
================================================

Every mod keeps its own license file in its folder. This file says where each
one comes from, under which licenses, and what goodluckOS leaves out or
replaces.

minetest_game: the game and every mod in mods/ not listed below
  https://github.com/luanti-org/minetest_game, commit @LUANTI_GAME_VERSION@
  Code LGPL-2.1+, media CC BY-SA 3.0 and others: LICENSE.txt and each mod's
  license.txt

mobs (mobs_redo)
  https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/mobs_redo, commit @MOBS_REDO_VERSION@
  Code MIT; media CC0 and CC BY-SA 3.0: mods/mobs/license.txt

mobs_animal
  https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/mobs_animal, commit @MOBS_ANIMAL_VERSION@
  Code MIT; media CC0, WTFPL, MIT, CC BY-SA 3.0: mods/mobs_animal/license.txt
  Replaced by Pixel Perfection's (see goodluck_models below): the cow's, the
  warthog's, the chickens' and the sheep's textures, for goodluck_models'
  meshes (the sheep's own four were CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial use only).
  Replaced by goodluckOS's own (see goodluck_* below): the rat's model and
  textures, credited only as "CCPL" with no version; and every file its
  credits don't name: the black and killer bunnies and the black kitten
  (recoloured from the credited grey and white bunnies, CC0, and the striped
  kitten, WTFPL), the egg icons, the cooked rat, and the beehive, butter,
  chicken, fried egg, feather, milk, hairball, honey and rabbit hide items.
  Left out: mobs_chicken_white.png, which nothing uses.

mobs_monster
  https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/mobs_monster, commit @MOBS_MONSTER_VERSION@
  Code MIT; media CC0, WTFPL, MIT, CC BY-SA 3.0 and 4.0:
  mods/mobs_monster/license.txt
  Replaced by goodluckOS's own: every file its credits don't name: the
  third dungeon master, land guards 4 to 8 and oerkki 2 and 3 (recoloured
  from the credited dungeon master and oerkki, WTFPL, and land guard, CC0),
  the cobweb, the lava pick, the sand particles, and the fire spirit (an
  empty image, as it was). Redrawn by goodluckOS: the stone monster's four
  textures, and the dirt and sand monsters' two each. mobs_spider.ogg is the
  sound mobs_animal's license.txt credits: Cyberpangolin, WTFPL. Added: an
  empty spawn.lua, so its monsters no longer spawn (goodluck_monsters spawns
  Minecraft's classic ones); they stay registered, their eggs out of the
  creative inventory.

stamina
  https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/stamina, commit @STAMINA_VERSION@
  Code LGPL-2.1+; media CC BY 3.0, WTFPL, CC0: mods/stamina/license.txt
  Left out: stamina_burp.ogg, from freesfx.co.uk, whose terms are not the
  CC BY 3.0 its credits give. It only plays for a drunk player, and nothing in
  this game makes one drunk.
  Replaced: stamina_hud_fg.png, stamina_hud_bg.png and stamina_hud_poison.png,
  by goodluckOS's own (see goodluck_* below).

mobs_npc
  https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/mobs_npc, commit @MOBS_NPC_VERSION@
  Code MIT; model CC BY-SA 3.0, textures CC BY-SA 3.0 and 4.0 and CC0:
  mods/mobs_npc/license.txt
  Replaced by goodluckOS's own: gui_arrow_blank.png and mobs_npc_shop_icon.png,
  which its credits don't name. Added: an empty spawn.lua, so mobs_npc spawns
  nothing by itself (goodluck_villages places the villagers), and the pt_BR
  translation. Its character model and skins ship unused: goodluck_villages
  puts Minecraft's villager on its NPC and trader (see goodluck_models).

3d_armor
  https://github.com/minetest-mods/3d_armor, commit @3D_ARMOR_VERSION@
  Only these mods of the modpack, without the .blend files: @3D_ARMOR_MODS@
  Code LGPL-2.1; armor textures CC BY-SA 3.0 (davidthecreator); sounds CC0:
  mods/3d_armor/LICENSE.md

x_enchanting
  https://bitbucket.org/minetest_gamers/x_enchanting, commit @X_ENCHANTING_VERSION@
  Code LGPL-2.1+; textures and models CC BY-SA 4.0 (some derived from
  CC BY-SA 3.0 and 4.0 ones, each named), sounds CC0, CC BY 3.0 and CC BY 4.0:
  mods/x_enchanting/LICENSE.txt
  Replaced by goodluckOS's own: x_enchanting_image_trade_1.png to _3.png, the
  enchanting table's cost, mese crystals there and lapis lazuli here. Added:
  the pt_BR translation.

x_bows
  https://bitbucket.org/minetest_gamers/x_bows, commit @X_BOWS_VERSION@
  Code LGPL-2.1+; textures and models CC BY-SA 4.0 and CC BY-SA 3.0, the
  arrows' flyby sounds CC0: mods/x_bows/LICENSE.txt
  Replaced by goodluckOS's own: the bow's sounds (drawing, fully drawn,
  shooting, the critical shot, the arrow hitting, the hit on a player), which
  its credits give to freesound.org authors with no particular Creative
  Commons license; and x_bows_bow_wood_semi_charged.png, which its credits
  don't name, redrawn from its x_bows_bow_wood.png and
  x_bows_bow_wood_charged.png and so under their CC BY-SA 4.0. Left out:
  the quiver's sounds (same credits as above; goodluck_bow takes the quiver
  out of the game) and smoke_puff.png (not credited, empty, unused). Added:
  the pt_BR translation. Its arrow model and x_bows_arrow_mesh.png ship
  unused: goodluck_models puts Minecraft's arrow on its arrow entity.

mob_horse
  https://codeberg.org/tenplus1/mob_horse, commit @MOB_HORSE_VERSION@
  Code MIT (TenPlus1); model mobs_horse.x MIT (Krupnov Pavel); sounds CC BY
  3.0: mob_horse.1.ogg by blazecon0 (freesound.org/people/blazecon0/sounds/99222),
  mob_horse.2.ogg by Gingerhoney (freesound.org/people/Gingerhoney/sounds/655070),
  mob_horse_whinny.ogg by GoodListener
  (freesound.org/people/GoodListener/sounds/322445): mods/mob_horse/license.txt
  and sounds/license.txt.
  Left out: its textures (the coats, CC BY-SA 3.0 by Mjollna, the horseshoes
  and the saddle overlay), as goodluck_horse wears goodluck_models' meshes
  and Pixel Perfection Legacy's coats (see goodluck_models) and takes the
  horseshoes out of the game, and spawn_example.lua; its model ships unused.
  Added: a spawn.lua that spawns nothing: goodluck_horse registers its own
  horse, donkey and mule with these sounds, and mob_horse's horse never
  appears.

goodluck_monsters
  The zombie, the skeleton and the spider are goodluck_models' meshes of
  Minecraft's, the spider with mobs_monster's mobs_spider.ogg (see
  mobs_monster); their skins, goodluck_monsters_zombie.png, _skeleton.png and
  _spider.png, are Pixel Perfection Legacy's (see goodluck_models), CC BY-SA
  4.0, the spider's with its eyes over it. Their arrow is goodluck_models'.
  The spider eye, goodluck_monsters_spider_eye.png, is Pixel Perfection
  Legacy's too (goodluckos/tools/art/potions.py), and so are the witch's skin and
  egg, goodluck_monsters_witch.png and _witch_egg.png (goodluckos/tools/art/witch.py),
  on goodluck_models' witch. The explosive mushroom's model, every other
  texture and the sounds are goodluckOS's own (see goodluck_* below).

goodluck_chest
  The big chest's halves, goodluck_chest_{front,top,back,inside}_{left,right}.png,
  are Minetest Game's default_chest_front, _top, _side and _inside.png (see
  Minetest Game, mods/default/license.txt) cut and joined by a script, and so
  under CC BY-SA 3.0. Its page numbers, goodluck_chest_page_1 and _2.png, are
  goodluckOS's own (see goodluck_* below).

goodluck_models
  Its meshes are goodluckOS's own, drawn by goodluckos/tools/art/models.py in the
  shapes Minecraft's textures take. goodluck_models_chest.png,
  _chest_left.png and _chest_right.png are Pixel Perfection by XSSheep, CC BY-SA
  4.0, as Mineclonia carries them (https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/mineclonia,
  commit 7410cf1, mods/ITEMS/mcl_chests; its LEGAL.md), turned to Minecraft's
  chest layout since 1.15 and the double one split into its halves by
  goodluckos/tools/art/pixelperfection.py, and so under CC BY-SA 4.0;
  goodluck_models_minecart.png and _minecart_item.png are XSSheep's minecart and
  its item from the same checkout (mods/ENTITIES/mcl_minecarts, CC BY-SA 3.0 by
  its README), unchanged. The bed's and the signs' textures are goodluckOS's own
  (goodluckos/tools/art/furniture.py, see goodluck_* below). The farm animals' meshes
  are goodluckOS's own too (goodluckos/tools/art/animals.py); their textures, under
  mobs_animal's names (mods/mobs_animal/textures: mobs_cow, mobs_cow2,
  mobs_pumba, mobs_chicken, _brown, _black, mobs_chick and mobs_sheep_base,
  _wool, _shaved, _horns), are Pixel Perfection's cow, pig, sheep, sheared
  sheep and wool by XSSheep and its chicken by kingoscargames, CC BY-SA 4.0, as
  the same checkout carries them (mods/ENTITIES/mobs_mc, its
  LICENSE-media.md), moved to Minecraft 26.x's layouts by
  goodluckos/tools/art/pixelperfection.py (the cow's muzzle taken from its nose, the
  wool made grey), and so under CC BY-SA 4.0; the horns are an empty image.
  The horses' and the spider's meshes are goodluckOS's own too (animals.py);
  the horses' textures, under goodluck_horse's names
  (mods/goodluck_horse/textures: the seven coats, the four markings, the
  donkey, the mule, the saddle, the chest and the horse armor), and the
  spider's skin (mods/goodluck_monsters/textures/goodluck_monsters_spider.png)
  are Pixel Perfection Legacy's by Nova Wostra, after XSSheep's Pixel
  Perfection, CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/pixel-perfection-legacy, 26.3-97.2), the
  horses' cut by pixelperfection.py into what each texture draws (the coats
  without the saddle and the chest, the saddle without the chest, the chest
  from the donkey's), the spider's eyes laid over it, and so under CC BY-SA
  4.0; the blank gear is an empty image. The zombie's, the skeleton's, the
  villager's, the boat's and the arrow's meshes are goodluckOS's own too
  (animals.py; the boat's and the arrow's take the place of boats' and
  x_bows' models); their textures, the zombie's and the skeleton's skins
  (goodluck_monsters), the villager's with its plains clothes and each job's
  over it (mods/goodluck_villages/textures: goodluck_villages_villager.png and
  _job_armorer, _toolsmith, _cleric, _librarian, _farmer), the oak boat
  (goodluck_models_boat.png) and the arrow (goodluck_models_arrow.png) are
  Pixel Perfection Legacy's (the villager's two laid together by
  pixelperfection.py), and so under CC BY-SA 4.0. The witch's mesh is
  goodluckOS's own too (animals.py), worn in Pixel Perfection Legacy's skin
  (see goodluck_monsters).

goodluck_villages
  Built on villages_mtg 1.0.3 by Drix03,
  https://content.luanti.org/packages/Drix03/villages_mtg/, MIT:
  mods/goodluck_villages/LICENSE-villages_mtg.md. Its eight buildings
  (schems/) are unchanged; the village code is reworked by goodluckOS. The
  villagers wear goodluck_models' villager, in Pixel Perfection Legacy's
  textures (see goodluck_models).

goodluck_recipes
  Its Recipes book is Minetest Game's mtg_craftguide, MIT (Jean-Patrick
  Guerrero and contributors, pauloue):
  mods/goodluck_recipes/LICENSE-mtg_craftguide.txt. Its code is reworked by
  goodluckOS to show what the player has learnt, with mtg_craftguide's strings
  and textures; the notice's box is goodluckOS's own (goodluckos/tools/art/toast.py,
  see goodluck_* below).

goodluck_nether

  by Nova Wostra, CC BY-SA 4.0, as Mineclonia 0.124.0 carries them
  (https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/mineclonia, mods/ITEMS/mcl_nether,
  mcl_blackstone, mcl_portals' portal and its particle, and mcl_core's bedrock,
  crying obsidian and its tear; its LEGAL.md), copied under goodluck_nether's
  names by goodluckos/tools/art/nether.py, and so under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its monsters
  (the ghast, the blaze, the magma cube, the zombified piglin and the wither
  skeleton) are goodluck_models' meshes of Minecraft's; their skins, their
  spawn eggs and the blaze rod, blaze powder, ghast tear, magma cream and fire
  charge are Pixel Perfection Legacy's by Nova Wostra (26.3-97.2,

  mob_spawner.png (above). Its code, its ruined portals' shapes and its
  fortresses, laid out as Minecraft's, are goodluckOS's own (see goodluck_*
  below).
